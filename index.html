Taehyeun Kim ORCID

Ph.D. Candidate · Aerospace Engineering

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Ilya Kolmanovsky and Prof. Anouck Girard.

My research focuses on safety-critical learning-based control, spacecraft rendezvous, proximity operations, and docking (RPOD), and cislunar navigation systems.

Research Interests
#

  • Safety-Critical Learning-based Control: Developing constrained control algorithms using numerical optimization and learning-based approaches
  • Spacecraft RPOD: Formulating and solving constrained control problems for on-orbit servicing missions
  • Cislunar Navigation: Designing position, navigation, and timing (PNT) constellation systems in cislunar space
